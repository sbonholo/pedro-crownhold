Crownhold

The King and Queen guard the Crown. Three warlords want it. Grow your realm in peace, and win the wars that come now and then.

Difficulty for a new realm
Map 1

How to play

First steps
  1. Village tab: press + to give peasants jobs.
  2. Build a Barracks in the Build tab.
  3. Train tab: train warriors.
  4. When raiders come, tap the Bell.
  5. War tab: march on a camp when your number is green.

Your goal

Keep the King, the Queen and the Crown safe inside the Keep.

Your realm never ends. Make your village, your army and your Renown stronger and stronger.

Resources

  • Wood from trees. Stone from grey rocks. Gold from shiny gold rocks. Food from hunting and Farms.
  • Open the Village tab and press + to give peasants a job. They walk, work and carry it home.
  • Hunters chase deer and boars and carry the meat home. Boars fight back: one hunter hunts deer, two or more take on boars.
  • Used-up trees, rocks and gold grow back after a while. Gold is short? Sell food or wood at the Market.
  • Tap trees, rocks, gold or a herd on the map and press Send: villagers work right there. Far spots are richer, but raiders pass by.
  • Build a Lumber Camp near trees, a Mining Camp near rocks and a Hunting Lodge near herds so trips are short.

What to do next

  • The goal button always shows your next goal. Tap it and it shows you how. A new realm starts with a short guide.
  • A grey card tells you what is missing. Tap it and it shows you where to get it.

Building

  • Pick a card in the Build tab, then tap a green spot on the map. Red means you can not build there.
  • Tap the Keep to grow your village: Hamlet, Village, Town, Castle. Each level brings more land, more room, a stronger Keep that shoots arrows, and new things to build. Tap a House, Farm, camp or Lodge to upgrade it too.
  • Houses give room for 5 more people. Archer Towers shoot arrows. Tap a tower to make it a Stone Tower (shoots farther and faster), then a Fortress Tower (2 archers, fire arrows).
  • Walls block raiders, but your own people can walk through them. Tap a wall to upgrade it: Wooden Palisade, Stone Wall (3 times the HP, needs a Village), Fortified Wall (6 times, with iron spikes, needs a Castle).

Warriors

  • Train warriors in the Train tab. Each one beats another kind:
  • Enemies have their own names. Raiders: send Archers. Brutes: send Swordsmen. Bowmen: send Knights. Riders: send Spearmen. Rams: send Swordsmen.
  • The Stable (needs a Town) trains riders on horses: Knights (strong), Horse Archers (shoot while riding) and Lancers (a gallop makes the first hit 2.5 times as hard). A horse takes 2 places, and Spearmen beat every horse.
  • Ranks and stars: in the Train tab, rank a warrior type up to Veteran (helmet, needs a Town) and Champion (cape, gold shield, needs a Castle) for more HP and damage. Each warrior also earns gold stars at 3, 8 and 15 kills.
  • Dragons fly over walls and breathe fire. Only arrows can hit them: Archers, Horse Archers, Archer Towers, the Keep and the Queen. Swordsmen, Spearmen and riders can not.
  • At Castle, build a Dragon Roost to hatch and grow your own dragon (Hatchling, Drake, Great Dragon). It guards the Keep and obeys the War tab. Later wars bring black dragons, and every camp has a sleeping guardian dragon.

Peace and war

  • Most of the time it is peace: no raids. Build, gather and train. The top bar shows when war comes.
  • The last minute of peace is a warning. Then a war season brings 2 or 3 raids. Beat them and peace returns with a reward chest.
  • In the War tab: Guard the Keep, Hold at flag, March on a camp, or Retreat to heal. You may march in peace too.
  • March when "Your army" is green: half again the camp's number. Amber means risky. A camp's dragon needs Archers.
  • Burn a camp for loot and Renown. It sits out the next war, then rises again one level stronger.
  • When raiders come, tap the big Bell button on the right: your peasants hide in the Keep. Tap All clear to send them back to work.
  • Raiders who lose most of their band break and run. Later wars bring tricks: a pincer from two sides, or wall-breakers that go for your weakest wall (it glows red).
  • After a war, tap the reward chest at the Keep gate and pick 1 of 3 spoils.

When things go wrong

  • If the Keep falls, a thief steals the Crown. Catch him, or burn his camp to win it back. Until then you gather 30% less.
  • If the King or Queen falls, they are wounded and rest in the Keep until they recover.
  • Renown from wars, camps and upgrades raises your title. Tap your title (top right) or open Records from the pause menu.

Shop

  • Build a Market, then open the Shop tab: trade wood, stone and food for gold (buying raises a price, selling lowers it), spend gold on potions, mercenaries and more, and catch the travelling merchant's deal.

Controls

  • Drag to move the map. Pinch or mouse wheel to zoom. Tap a card, then tap the map.
  • Turn the view: twist two fingers, press Q or E, or tap the turn buttons under the map. The arrow button faces north again.
  • Keys: WASD or arrows move, Q and E turn, 1 to 5 switch tabs, Space pauses, Esc cancels.
  • Graphics: pick Low, Medium or High in the pause menu. Low runs best on older phones.
