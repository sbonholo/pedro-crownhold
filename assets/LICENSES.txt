CAVEATS
-------
- EZ-Tree leaf textures: distributed under the package's MIT licence, but the package gives no upstream
  source for the four leaf PNGs. If you need a fully traceable chain, replace the leaf card texture.
- Spruit Sunrise HDRI shows modern electricity pylons on the horizon (fine for lighting; avoid as visible sky).
- No asset here copies a specific existing game's characters or look; KayKit and Quaternius are generic
  original CC0 packs.
